
## Contrast 2 — ergative cue → verb aspect

Δ = logit(perfective, agreeing with object) − logsumexp(habitual masc, habitual fem). Expect Δ>0 after मैंने (clean) and Δ<0 after मैं (corrupt).

**hi clean** (n=40): mean Δ = 1.553 [0.471, 2.608], acc = 0.575 [0.425, 0.725], mean P(verb prefix)=0.075  

**hi corrupt** (n=40): mean Δ = -2.686 [-3.180, -2.196], acc = 0.925 [0.825, 1.000], mean P(verb prefix)=0.069  

**hi paired** Δ_clean − Δ_corrupt (n=40): 4.239 [3.235, 5.204], frac>0 = 0.925  

**pa control** ਮੈਂ O (n=12): mean Δ = -1.506 [-2.395, -0.585]
